Abstract

We present a motion stitching method for smoothly connecting multiple motion clips with large pose differences. Our method represents joint rotations using Rodrigues vectors and formulates motion stitching as a Laplacian optimization problem. By smoothing transition regions while preserving the local temporal variations of each motion, the method synthesizes natural motion sequences without relying on a large blending window. We also analyze the effect of discontinuities in the Rodrigues vector representation and confirm that the proposed method can be stably applied to real motion data.
